# Kisan Sathi User Personas

This document describes the key user personas targeting the Kisan Sathi platform to align product UX and functional scope.

## Persona A — Small Farmer (P0 Priority)
*   **Characteristics:** Small landholding (< 2 acres), limited technology familiarity, possibly low literacy, strong regional language preference.
*   **Connectivity:** Poor/intermittent cellular network.
*   **Needs:** Highly visual, simple actionable information, voice queries, minimal text inputs.
*   **MVP Focus:** Onboarding via photo, crop tracking alerts, simple voice chat.

## Persona B — Medium Farmer (P0/P1 Priority)
*   **Characteristics:** 2 to 10 acres, moderate tech literacy, uses smartphones daily.
*   **Needs:** Planning, profitability estimation, resource optimization, weather-based crop risk alerts.
*   **MVP Focus:** Crop tracking calendar, smart weather-adjusted warnings, disease diagnostics.

## Persona C — Large / Progressive Farmer (P2 Priority)
*   **Characteristics:** > 10 acres, multi-crop/multi-farm, high tech literacy, data-driven.
*   **Needs:** Advanced analytics, soil health historical data, satellite/sensor tracking, market price optimization.

## Persona D — Agricultural Expert (P1 Priority)
*   **Characteristics:** Certified agronomist or extension officer, web dashboard user.
*   **Needs:** Expert review queue for low-confidence AI disease reports, farmer query management.

## Persona E — Content / Agriculture Administrator (P1 Priority)
*   **Characteristics:** App system administrator, coordinates content.
*   **Needs:** Course creation tools, translation verifications, government schemes registry moderation.

## Persona F — Local Buyer / Trader (P0/P1 Priority)
*   **Characteristics:** Mandi commission agent (Arhtiya), private regional trader, or local procurement officer. Highly tech-savvy, uses WhatsApp for price broadcasts.
*   **Needs:** Direct access to regional crop volume availability, active price broadcasting tools, and verified farmer contact info.
*   **MVP Focus:** Posting active buying offers (variety, price, grade requirements) and browsing farmer crop availability logs.
